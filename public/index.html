Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about YouTube creator discovery, safety guidelines, and data accuracy.

How does Discover mode find channels without a video link?

In Discover mode, our engine queries YouTube Data API directly for active channels categorized under your selected niche (Tech, Gaming, Cooking, etc.) and filters them by your chosen subscriber thresholds (e.g. 1k – 10k) and country.

What does the [Visit] button do?

Clicking [Visit] directly opens the creator's official YouTube channel in a new tab so you can immediately view their latest videos, comment, or subscribe.

How can I be sure the creator is actually from the US or UK?

Our tool directly checks the official country field registered in YouTube Studio by the channel owner. In Strict Mode, only channels officially verified in the US, UK, Canada, Australia, or Germany are returned.

How do I safely mention creators without getting spam penalties?

Never paste 30-40 handles in a single comment. Use our "Safe Description Shoutout" box (limiting to 5-7 creators per video) or "Safe Batches" (3-5 handles per comment) so YouTube never flags your account as spam.
